How it works

Every night, your child receives a thoughtfully-crafted, open-ended question designed to spark reflection and build confidence. Each question is themed around a different confidence-building topic— courage, kindness, resilience, creativity, and more.

Unlike yes/no questions, these open-ended prompts invite your child to share their thoughts, feelings, and experiences, helping them recognize their own strengths and positive qualities.

What makes this special:

  • One new question per night at your chosen time
  • Themes rotate through 12 confidence-building topics
  • Personalized with your child's name in the email
  • Parent-facing tips explain why each question matters
  • Mobile-optimized emails, beautifully designed
